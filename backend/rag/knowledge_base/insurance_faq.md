# Insurance & Billing FAQ

## BlueShield Basic
Covers 80% of outpatient visits. Does not cover dental or vision. Annual
deductible of $500 applies before coverage begins.

## HealthFirst Plus
Covers 100% of outpatient visits, including dental and vision. No annual
deductible. Pre-authorization required for specialist referrals.

## Claim Processing Time
Standard claims are processed within 10-14 business days. Denied claims can
be appealed within 30 days of the denial notice.

## Co-pay Collection
Co-pays are collected at time of visit for all plans except HealthFirst
Plus, which bills the practice directly.
