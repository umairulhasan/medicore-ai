# Clinic Policies

## Cancellation Policy
Patients must cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before their appointment
to avoid a $25 late-cancellation fee.

## Walk-in Policy
Walk-ins are accepted for urgent, non-life-threatening concerns only, on a
first-come basis, subject to same-day slot availability.

## New Patient Requirements
New patients must complete an intake form and provide a valid photo ID and
insurance card (if applicable) before their first visit.

## Prescription Refill Policy
Refill requests must be submitted at least 3 business days before the
current prescription runs out. Controlled substances require an in-person
visit and cannot be refilled via chat or phone.
